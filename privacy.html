Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-12

1. Data komugi accesses

Only data about the operator's own cosme_intro accounts: basic profile (display name, avatar), account statistics (follower, following, like and video counts) and the list of the operator's own videos with their view, like, comment and share counts.

2. How the data is used

The data is used only to let the operator review the performance of their own videos.

3. Storage

Access tokens and statistics are stored on the operator's own computers. Tokens are kept in local configuration files that are not published.

4. Sharing

The data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

5. Other users

komugi does not collect any data about other TikTok or Instagram users.

6. Deletion

The operator can revoke komugi's access at any time from the TikTok or Instagram app settings; stored data is then deleted.

7. Contact
